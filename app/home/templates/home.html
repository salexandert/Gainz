Gainz

Offline crypto accounting and cost-basis reconciliation

Gainz helps you import exchange CSVs, link sale records to earlier buys, review unresolved transfers, and export documentation for review with a qualified tax professional.

The clearest path is to import what you know, declare what you currently hold, reconcile the difference, then review generated reports and export. Gainz runs locally so your transaction history stays on this machine.

Gainz does not provide legal, financial, or tax advice. Validate reports and source records before filing.
