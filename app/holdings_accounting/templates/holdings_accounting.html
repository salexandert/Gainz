Holdings & Accounting Walkthrough

Use this page to record what you currently hold, compare that against imported activity, and identify records that may need review.

Click a summary card to focus the asset list and see the next step.
1
Import what you know

Start with exchange CSVs and any manual transactions you already know about. Transfers do not need perfect wallet mapping; Gainz uses one universal wallet for basis.

Import CSVs Add manual transactions
2
Declare current holdings

Select each asset below and enter the amount you want Gainz to use for reconciliation, based on your exchange, wallet, and custody records.

3
Compare the two stories

Calculated net from imported buys/sells asks: based only on imported buy and sell records, how much should remain? Your declared holdings help identify records that may need review.

4
Resolve gaps deliberately

If calculated holdings differ from declared holdings, review source records for missing acquisitions, disposals, transfers, losses, income, gifts, or other activity. Reclassify only when supported by documentation.

5
Verify on Stats

Once assets are declared and review items are handled, review Current Holdings Reconciliation, current lots, unlinked sales, and audit packet readiness.
